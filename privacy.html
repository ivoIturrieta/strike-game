STRIKE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Strike is a game made by Ivo Iturrieta. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. It works offline. To learn how the game is played, it sends anonymous play stats, which you can turn off.

What the play stats are. When your iPhone is online, Strike sends short notes about play to PostHog, an analytics service: which stages you start, win or lose and how long they take, how strong your squad was, which story pages you read or skip, what you build and train at the camp, whether THE WHOLE ROAD or PAINT TINS was offered, bought or restored, which settings you change, and how smoothly the game runs (how long it takes to start, its frame rate, and whether it had to restart). PostHog also notes when the app is opened and closed, the kind of iPhone and its iOS version, its screen size, language and time zone, and a random number made for this copy of the app.

Anonymous. No name, no email, no account, no advertising ID and nothing you type is sent. The random number is not linked to you. As with any connection, your iPhone's internet address reaches PostHog with the play stats; Strike tells PostHog not to use it to look up where you are, so your location is not looked up. The play stats are used only to make Strike better; they are never sold, never shared with anyone else and never used for advertising or tracking. When you are offline, they wait on your iPhone and go later.

How to turn them off. In the camp, open SETTINGS (the gear under your scrap) and tap SHARE ANONYMOUS PLAY STATS so it says OFF. From then on Strike sends no play stats, until you turn it back on.

What stays on your iPhone. Your progress (your camp, your rigs, how far up the road you are, and your settings) is saved on your iPhone. It never leaves it and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases. THE WHOLE ROAD and PAINT TINS are bought through Apple. Strike never sees your payment details or who you are; Apple tells the game only which purchases you own.

Children. Strike doesn't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children. The play stats above are the same anonymous ones for every player.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
